**Local Flow Trail Memory**

A future feature for Natural Math where successful movement leaves a temporary positive trail, like ant pheromones, veins, or a slide/rail path.

Core idea:

```text
pressure scars = avoid bad/blocked cells
flow trails = prefer successful paths
```

Minimal rule:

```text
trail[edge] *= decay

if move_successful:
    trail[current_cell -> next_cell] += deposit

score(candidate) =
    alignment_with_current_direction
    - scar_weight * pressure_scar[candidate]
    + trail_weight * trail[current_cell -> candidate]
```

Important constraints:

- Only local adjacent moves are scored.
- Trails decay over time.
- Trails cannot move through walls.
- Trails do not use BFS, A*, global maps, or stored routes.
- Edge trails are better than cell trails because they reinforce “move from here to there,” like rails or veins.
- This should be tested as a future feature, not mixed into v4.0.1 cleanup.

Purpose:

To create self-reinforcing lanes, bridges, or flow paths that nodes can follow naturally after repeated successful movement.
