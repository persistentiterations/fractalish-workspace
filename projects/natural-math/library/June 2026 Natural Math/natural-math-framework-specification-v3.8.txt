### III. Core Theoretical Pillars

2.  **Information Locality (Theorem 2):** Information cannot travel faster than the sensing radius $r$ per step. This creates a hard physical constraint on how nodes gain "knowledge" of their surroundings, preventing the "global oversight" that invalidates many existing ABM models.
3.  **Local Agency (Principle 2):** Every state transition $\Phi(S_t)$ is computed using only local information. This mirrors biological processes where cells do not calculate the "goal" of the entire organism, but respond solely to local gradients.
