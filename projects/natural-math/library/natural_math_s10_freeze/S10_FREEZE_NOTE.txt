KNOWN CAVEAT
------------
The analyzer reported ScreamOnSuccessEdges total=24 during the partial
ledger run. This was traced to 12 passing rows with 2 contaminated edges
each. The contamination is small, bounded, and did not cause any failures.
It was not fully resolved before tool budget ended. It is recorded as an
analyzer/ledger caveat rather than a blocker to freezing Stage 10.

Root cause of residual contamination:
  - Scream is written during scout exploration (when cycles are detected)
  - Success edges are registered AFTER the scout completes the cycle
  - The immunity check prevents FUTURE writes on success edges but does
    not retroactively remove scream written before success was recorded
  - Fix options: (a) accept as known residual, (b) add retroactive cleanup
    after each scout cycle, (c) add per-scout success tracking

CORRECTIONS APPLIED TO S10-5 BASELINE
---------------------------------------
1. AUDIT_TIMEOUT = MAX_STEPS = 500
   Reason: Old timeout of 200 was shorter than actual 20x20 route lengths.

2. Success-edge write immunity in write_scream_edge:
   If an edge is in scout_success_edges_out or scout_success_edges_home,
   do not write cycle-scream onto that edge.
   Reason: Prevents recontamination death spiral where later scouts
   write scream onto edges proven successful by earlier scouts.
