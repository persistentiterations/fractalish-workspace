What to Watch

short guide
Gate first. No manifest produces HOLD. Risk or tamper produces RETRACT. Only a clean matching manifest unlocks the actual field run.
Scout earns memory. The scout moves one cell at a time, makes wrong turns, leaves messy seen marks, and only confirms the final out-and-home corridor after it returns to base.
Follower is stricter. It ignores raw seen marks and moves only on the confirmed corridor. Repeating the same maze/resource reuses the corridor; changing either forces fresh discovery.
