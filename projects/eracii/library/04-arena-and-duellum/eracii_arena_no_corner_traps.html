Meet the Legends

These are original abstract medallions, not copied portraits. Each card demonstrates a different movement system and category identity.

How to Play

1. MoveClick your figure, then a highlighted space. Each legend has a unique movement map.
2. ActChoose one of the four large buttons after moving.
3. Read the rivalThe rival's current leaning is visible above their card.
4. Break AwayIf the rival blocks every route, gold escape tiles let you move past them. Neither player can lose a turn by being cornered.
5. WinReduce the rival's Resolve to zero or lead after fourteen rounds.

The same arena and the same choices always produce the same result.
