District of Intent

You are carrying a record through a district under pressure. At three gates you may EXTEND, HOLD, or RESTRICT. The choice changes the physical route, the people who remember it, and what reaches the final council.

No ordinary random calls are used. The same seed and the same input ticks replay the same run. Small changes in timing, position, witnesses, conversations, and collected evidence can change the ending even when the three headline choices match.

Cause becomes terrainBridges, gates, crowds, and access routes physically change after your intervention.
Intent stays visiblePeople show what they are preparing to do before they act.
The full cost waitsHidden state is revealed at the council through a receipt chain and outcome portrait.

The prototype is intentionally small: one district, seven people, three terrain mutations, one Witness companion, a final council, and exact input replay.
