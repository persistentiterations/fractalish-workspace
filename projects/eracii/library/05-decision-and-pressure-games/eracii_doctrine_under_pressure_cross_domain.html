What this experiment is

Doctrine Under Pressure tests the universal gap between stated values and real-world compromise. A doctrine can represent an AI system prompt, a corporate charter, a constitutional rule set, or a person's declared ethics. The environment is the reality check that restricts its options.

Translation: doctrine = the rules we swear by; environment = the stress test; live state = what resources and options remain; contradiction = the moment behavior compromises the stated rule; receipt chain = the decision paper trail.

It is intentionally separate from Eracii Arena / Trial of Minds. This experiment has no historical figures, combat, or manual movement.

Every decision stores the observed state, candidate scores, accessibility exclusions, chosen action, state transition, previous receipt hash, and resulting receipt hash.
