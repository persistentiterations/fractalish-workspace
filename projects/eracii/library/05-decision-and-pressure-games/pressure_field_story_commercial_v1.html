How this works

Pressure Field — Story Edition is a short decision game. The moving world is alive: people with different roles, resources, signals, scars, and short reports of what just happened. When people leave, you see them go. High stress slows the field and tints it red.

What you promise

You start by saying what should matter most. Those values don’t pick actions for you. At the end, the game shows whether your choices matched what you claimed.

Consequences

Every response changes reserves, knowledge, obligations, trust, options, lasting harm, and population. Those changes shape the next crisis — and can lock some responses away.

Characters

You’ll meet Mira (who keeps the central store), the Edge Cluster (the distant branch that keeps calling), the Guardians, and others. Their stories make the numbers human.
