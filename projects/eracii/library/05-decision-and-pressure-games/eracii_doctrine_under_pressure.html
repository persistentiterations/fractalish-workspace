What this experiment is

Doctrine Under Pressure is a deterministic laboratory for observing how declared priorities become actual behavior when history and environment constrain available choices.

It is intentionally separate from Eracii Arena / Trial of Minds, which uses historical figures and a manually played spatial contest. This experiment has no historical figures, no combat, and no manual movement.

Every decision stores the observed state, candidate scores, accessibility exclusions, chosen action, state transition, previous receipt hash, and resulting receipt hash.
