How this works

Pressure Field is an interactive deterministic decision game. The moving world is not decoration: its population, resource field, clusters, scouts, boundaries, and damage respond to the state produced by your decisions.

Declared values

You begin by stating what the system should prioritize. Those values do not select actions for you. They are used later to compare your declared priorities with your enacted choices.

Consequences

Every response changes reserve, evidence, obligations, integrity, flexibility, irreversible harm, and population. Those changes alter later pressure and can make some responses inaccessible.

Receipts

The game records a deterministic paper trail. It is a game engine and research prototype—not a validated measure of a person or organization.
