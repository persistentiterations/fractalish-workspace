Everyday parallel

The bus is supposed to run on time. When it does, you barely notice. When it’s late, the cascade starts:

Late bus → Missed meeting → Rushed decision → Costly error

Garbage collection is the same: one skipped route and the street fills, then the complaint, then the overtime, then the budget fight.

Stated rules (“we put safety first”) are the schedule. Pressure is the late bus. What you actually do under scarcity is the real doctrine — and it propagates downstream just like a missed pickup.
