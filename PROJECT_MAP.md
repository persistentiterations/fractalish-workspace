# Project Map

## Natural Math

Path: `projects/natural-math/`

Mathematical framework, CNTM implementations, experiments, oracle runners, persistence work, reports, and specifications. This is the largest research family and requires a canonical-version review.

## Guardian

Path: `projects/guardian/`

Code-intake, verification, trust, release, and Windows-builder work. Generated build material was excluded where recognized.

## Eracii

Path: `projects/eracii/`

HTML games, world and homestead prototypes, villages and districts, arena and duellum experiments, design documents, and decision games.

## Bolt-On

Path: `projects/bolt-on/`

Demonstrator, compatibility, continuity, freeze, verification, and staged release work.

## SymLan

Path: `projects/symlan/`

Symbolic-language, governed-persistence, graph, and teaching-package work.

## Fractalish Core

Path: `projects/fractalish-core/`

Cross-cutting architecture and central Fractalish papers that are not limited to one implementation family.

## Universal

Path: `universal/`

Material that crosses multiple named projects and should not be forced into one project until reviewed.

## Reports

Path: `reports/`

- `included-files.csv`: copied files and SHA-256 hashes.
- `exact-duplicates-not-copied.csv`: exact repeats and the retained copy.
- `excluded-files.csv`: generated, archived, installer, or oversized material that remains in the organized local library.
- `build-summary.json`: staging statistics.
