# Claim boundary

## Supported claim

> On 16 deterministic constructed cases, counting supplied causal dependency
> roots avoided the false establishments produced by message, relay, and
> declared-provenance counting.

## Not supported

This experiment does not show that causal dependencies can always be inferred
from real evidence. The fixtures supply dependency paths and expected answers.
The program validates counting, conflict handling, cycle rejection, and
decision behavior after those labels are available.

It is not evidence that Natural Math as a whole is correct. It is an isolated
evidence-governance mechanism potentially usable by Natural Math, Guardian,
HOLD, Bolt-On, or multi-agent review.

## Next evidence needed

Dependency roots must be earned from captured runtime lineage or independently
audited provenance. A self-declared dependency label must not receive authority
merely because it has the correct field name.
