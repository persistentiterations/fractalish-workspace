Eracii × SymLan Convergence ArenaDesign Brief v0.1 — Team Review PackageStatus: Design brief for review (not a runtime, not a live feature)  Date: 2026-07-28  Authors: Synthesis from ongoing SymLan arena work + Eracii live surfaces  Related accepted artifacts:`SymLan_Spatial_Temporal_HAL_Profile_v0.1.md` (projected 3-space)`SymLan_Attractor_Graph_HAL_Profile_v0.1.md` (attractor graph)`materializer_v0_1.py` (both profiles)Hotseat play surface + session receipts (board path)Eracii live: eracii.com (602 cards), /arena, /duelOne-line purpose:  Define a playable, multi-profile strategy surface in which a SymLan-generated attractor graph is the shared space, Eracii dossiers (or teams) are the actors, and convergence is an inspectable graph condition — not hit points.---1. Why this brief existsChess and slower strategy games are returning among the late-teen / early-20s circle around the project. Eracii already holds a broad spectrum of humanity (602 dossiers across foundations, military, philosophy, mind, medicine, spiritual, stage/screen, inventors, sports, pop, K-pop, digital creators). Duel already proves deterministic, Natural Math–informed, inspectable play.What is still missing is a surface where:The space itself is generated from SymLan (earned vocabulary as places, couplings as paths).Multiple human profiles can occupy that space at once.Pressure arrives from multiple directions and resolves (or HOLDs) under a shared ternary gate.The result stays continuous with Eracii’s identity: historical minds in an arena, not a fantasy reboot.This brief packages that design for team review before any further build.---2. Design law (inherited)> The programmer specifies the arena. The substrate earns the vocabulary.  > Symbols are usable only after resolution. HOLD is legal. False closure is not.Graph consequence: the walkable places are not a free skill menu. Vertices that come from V appear only after resolve. Mock discovery must stay labeled mock in prototypes.---3. Geometry (authority)Primary geometry for this surface:  `spatial_temporal.attractor_graph.v0.1`Element	MeaningVertices	Resolved vocabulary states (V-loci) + surviving structural lociEdges	Couplings that remain legal after resolve + gate-permitted send pathsMovement	Stay on a vertex, or traverse one legal directed edgeGate	SymLan proceed / hold / reverse (not Basin action states)Layout	Optional human drawing only; never the rule surfaceProjected 3-space remains the complementary teaching/board surface. It is not discarded. Dual-profile fusion is out of scope for v0.1 of this arena.---4. Multi-profile / dossier model4.1 ActorsEach human profile / user claims one actor locus (or a small fixed team of loci) on the shared graph.An actor locus is bound to an Eracii dossier (or a neutral structural id for early prototypes).The dossier supplies flavor, starting stance, and optional once-per-match special framing.  The rules remain SymLan: stay, traverse, send under the gate.4.2 Shared graphOne materializer output (one SymLan program + seed + profile) produces the graph for the match.All profiles stand on the same vertex set and edge skeleton.Multiple actors may occupy different vertices at once.  v0.1 default: one actor per vertex (no stacking) unless a later rule explicitly allows it.4.3 Superposition (honest, limited)“Superposition” here does not mean quantum mechanics or hidden boards.It means:Several legal actions may be pending across different actors.A shared (or per-focus-pair) ternary gate decides what may commit.Until the gate allows a commit, those paths are not pretended to be resolved.This is the same discipline as pending → resolved vocabulary, applied to multi-actor pressure.---5. Verbs (human-facing)Under the current gate, an actor may:Verb	EffectStay	Locus unchanged. Always legal. Preferred under HOLD.Traverse	Move along one legal directed edge to a new vertex.Send	Offer a resolved symbol along a gate-legal path. Does not teleport the actor by default.Gate effects (aligned with Attractor-Graph HAL §5):Gate	Stay	Traverse	Sendproceed	Allowed	Allowed on legal edges	Allowedhold	Allowed (preferred)	Stay-only (v0.1 default)	Blockedreverse	Allowed	Allowed	Self-recoil (no free progress on target)No additional combat stats required for the first playable loop.---6. Convergence (win / hold / close conditions)Convergence is an inspectable graph condition, not a private score.v0.1 candidates (team chooses one primary for the first build):Shared expect — Two or more actors successfully complete an `expect` of the same symbol at a declared target within the match window.Formation complete — A declared set of edges is traversed or held in a recorded pattern.Coherence budget — A shared step index S reaches a limit under mutual pressure; final gate state and loci are the result.Mutual HOLD — All active actors pass under HOLD for a declared number of rounds; the graph state at that moment is the outcome.Receipts record the path. The log is part of the game, not debug chrome.---7. What the human seesMinimum readable surface:Current locus of each actor (dossier name + vertex id)Legal edges from the current locus under the active gateGate outcome + short tipEvent log (stay / traverse / send / blocked / recoil) with receiptsWhose turn (or simultaneous commit window, later)Convergence progress (simple indicator toward the chosen condition)No requirement for 3D camera, fog of war, or animated combat in v0.1.---8.
